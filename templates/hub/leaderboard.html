TRANSPARENT ACADEMIC MERIT SYSTEM

How to Earn StudySphere Points

StudySphere rewards meaningful academic contributions rather than message volume. Help your classmates, share knowledge, and grow your campus learning community.

Activity Reward Conditions
📤 Share Study Notes +25 pts Published and approved in the community library (automatically revoked if hidden for inaccuracy).
⭐ Resource Review +15 pts Valid peer rating (1–5 stars) and written review on a study material.
🎓 Solve Academic Doubt +20 pts Accepted solution in the Academic Doubts forum (verified by the question author or a moderator).
✅ Complete Material +10 pts Completed study material scheduled for spaced-repetition review.
✨ Complete AI Quiz +10 pts Valid completed practice quiz attempt in AI Study Studio.
💬 General Talk 0 pts No points — casual group chat messages never inflate leaderboard scores.
FAIRNESS & INTEGRITY

Anti-Spam & Daily Reward Limits

  • One Reward per Academic Doubt: Only the single accepted solution earns +20 points. Asking a doubt or posting ordinary unaccepted replies awards 0 points.
  • No Self-Answer Rewards: Students cannot accept their own replies or earn points on their own doubts.
  • Meaningful Explanations Required: Accepted solutions must contain at least 15 characters and 3 distinct words.
  • Daily Doubt-Solving Cap: Maximum {{ max_daily_doubt_rewards|default:5 }} rewarded solutions ({{ max_daily_doubt_points|default:100 }} points) per student per day.
  • Anti-Collusion Protection: Repeated solution rewards between the same two students are automatically rate-limited.
  • Moderator Audit & Revocation: Staff moderators can revoke fraudulent rewards; all awards and deductions remain permanently visible in your Points History ledger.
